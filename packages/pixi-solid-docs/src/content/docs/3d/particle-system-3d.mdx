`pixi-solid-3d` provides the `<ParticleSystem3D>` component for rendering high-performance GPU-instanced particle effects like fire, sparks, smoke plumes, rain, dust, and explosions.

### Why `<ParticleSystem3D>` in SolidJS?

In `@pixi/3d`, `ParticleSystem3D` defaults to binding its simulation step to Pixi's global `Ticker.shared`.

`<ParticleSystem3D>`:

1. **Context Ticker Synchronization**: Automatically synchronizes particle simulation with the nearest contextual Pixi ticker (from `<PixiCanvas>`, `<PixiApplicationProvider>`, or `<TickerProvider>`) instead of global timers.
2. **Fine-Grained Reactivity**: Reactive props (`emitRate`, `gravity`, `drag`, `playing`, `frameRate`) update live emitters without tearing down GPU buffers.
3. **Automated Lifecycle**: When the component unmounts, particle GPU buffers, instanced quad arrays, and ticker bindings are cleaned up automatically.

### Emitter Shapes

Emitter shapes come directly from `@pixi/3d/extras`:

- `ConeShape({ angle, radius })` — Spawns on an XZ disc and shoots particles upwards in a cone. Perfect for fire plumes, exhaust, fountains, and torches.
- `SphereShape({ radius, surfaceOnly })` — Spawns within or on the surface of a sphere, shooting radially outward. Ideal for magic orbs, explosions, and bursts.
- `BoxShape({ width, height, depth })` — Spawns across a 3D volume. Ideal for ambient dust motes, snow, and rain.
- `PointShape()` — Spawns all particles from a single origin point.

### Spreads: Ranges vs. Fixed Numbers

Properties like `speed`, `life`, `size`, `sizeEnd`, `alpha`, and `alphaEnd` accept **spreads**:

- **Fixed scalar**: `speed={4}` (all particles spawn with speed 4)
- **Range `[min, max]`**: `speed={[2, 5]}` (randomized between 2 and 5 for each particle)

```tsx
<ParticleSystem3D
  capacity={2000}
  emitRate={80}
  shape={new ConeShape({ angle: 25, radius: 0.2 })}
  speed={[3, 6]}
  life={[1.0, 2.0]}
  size={[0.3, 0.6]}
  sizeEnd={0.05} // Shrinks as it dies
  color={0xff6600}
  colorEnd={0x220000} // Fades to dark red
  alphaEnd={0} // Fades out completely
  blendMode="add" // Additive glow
/>
```

### Additive Blending vs Normal

- `blendMode="add"`: Particles brighten whatever is behind them. Best for fire, glowing sparks, laser exhaust, and magic.
- `blendMode="normal"`: Standard alpha compositing. Best for smoke, dust, leaves, and opaque debris.
